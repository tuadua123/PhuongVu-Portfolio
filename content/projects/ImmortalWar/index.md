## Overview

Immortal Wars: Battler RPG is a PvP game where you can summon warriors of legends to your party, fight and defeat dark enemies.

- Genre: RPG Card Game
- Engine: Unity
- Platform: Android

Major tasks and responsibilities 
 
- Prepoduction: Character concept arts
- Production: Redesign UI
- Tool used: Figma, Photoshop, Illustrator
